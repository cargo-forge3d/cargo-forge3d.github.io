CargoForge: Generative 3D Reconstruction for Port Breakbulk Cargo

CargoForge turns non-contact port cargo photos into reusable GLB/PBR mesh assets. It adapts Hunyuan3D-2.1 for port breakbulk cargo, accelerates the shape stage with HiCache++, and serves shape/texture generation through a dual-GPU asynchronous inference pipeline.

Why this project exists

From single-image demos to port-grade asset generation

01

Fast white-mesh reconstruction

HiCache++ reuses historical velocity fields during DiT sampling and skips part of the expensive forward passes. In typical RTX 4080 tests, 30-step shape sampling dropped from 40s to 7s, reducing end-to-end shape latency from 67.3s to 24.0s.

02

Dual-GPU asynchronous serving

A FastAPI service uses Redis queues and PostgreSQL job state to split shape and texture into two GPU-resident workers. GPU0 handles shape; GPU1 handles texture; the system records status, events, artifacts, queue pressure, VRAM, CPU/RAM, and failures.

03

Domain LoRA adaptation

Teacher GLB assets from a stronger commercial Hunyuan3D model are converted into watertight mesh, SDF samples, and multi-view render conditions. Rank-16 LoRA improves validation Chamfer-L1 by 36.0% and F-score@0.05 by 12.3 percentage points.

Domain adaptation

Teacher-supervised LoRA for port cargo geometry

Hunyuan3D-2.1 is open-source but weak on long, stacked, and irregular port cargo. CargoForge builds a teacher-labeled cargo dataset and adapts the shape model with LoRA.
